    A LITTLE GUIDE TO GROWING

    How to Grow Your Happiness Tree

    Every month, a new seed begins its happiness journey. Record your mood and reflect on your day to help it grow.

    1. Choose one of five moods to remember how a happy moment felt.
    2. Earn +5 HP for your first daily mood check-in.
    3. Write about happy moments to earn reflection and bonus HP.
    4. Collect HP to grow your monthly Happiness Tree.
    5. Revisit your memories through the Happiness Calendar.
    • ☀
      Daily Mood Check-in +5 HPChoose Happy, Grateful, Calm, Excited, or Neutral to remember how a happy moment felt. Save without writing if you wish. All five earn the same daily reward.
    • ✎
      Daily Reflection +10 HPWrite and save a reflection. Awarded once per day, separately from your mood check-in.
    • ✧
      Positive Words +5 HP eachEach unique recognized positive word per day. Words can earn points again tomorrow.
    • ✿
      Happy Memory +10 HPCheck the happy memory box and write at least 30 characters. Once per day.
    • ♡
      Gratitude Reflection +10 HPWrite a reflection and choose Grateful, check gratitude, or express gratitude in your text. Once per day.

    You get one tree each month. It reaches full size at 600 HP, and you can keep earning points for the rest of the month.

    You can reflect more than once a day. Every mood is welcome, and difficult feelings never take points away.
