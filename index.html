    A LITTLE GUIDE TO GROWING

    How to Grow Your Happiness Tree

    Every month, a new Happiness Tree begins growing. Collect Happiness Points by recording your moods and happy moments.

    1. Choose one of five moods to remember how a happy moment felt.
    2. Earn +5 HP for your first daily mood check-in.
    3. Write about happy moments to earn reflection and bonus HP.
    4. Collect HP throughout the month to grow your Happiness Tree.
    5. Reach 1,000 HP to fully grow your monthly Happiness Tree.
    6. Revisit your memories through the Happiness Calendar.
    • ☀
      Daily Mood Check-in +5 HPChoose Happy, Grateful, Calm, Excited, or Neutral to remember how a happy moment felt. Save without writing if you wish. All five earn the same daily reward.
    • ✎
      Daily Reflection +10 HPWrite and save a reflection. Awarded once per day, separately from your mood check-in.
    • ✧
      Positive Words +2 HP eachMaximum 3 unique recognized positive words per day (+6 HP). Words can earn points again tomorrow.
    • ✿
      Happy Memory +7 HPCheck the happy memory box and write at least 30 characters. Once per day.
    • ♡
      Gratitude Reflection +8 HPWrite a reflection and choose Grateful, check gratitude, or express gratitude in your text. Once per day.

    Your monthly Happiness Tree can earn up to 1,000 HP. Once you reach 1,000 HP, your tree is fully grown! New mood check-ins and reflections pause until the next calendar month. Your saved diary entries and memories remain available to view.

    Maximum daily activity reward: 36 HP (5 mood + 10 reflection + 6 positive words + 7 happy memory + 8 gratitude). A fresh tree and a new 1,000 HP allowance begin each calendar month.

    You can reflect more than once a day. Every mood is welcome, and difficult feelings never take points away.
